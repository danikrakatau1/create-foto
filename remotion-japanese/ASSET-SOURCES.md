## Motion lock note

The V1.1 cinematic direction keeps Fuji, pagoda, and the core landscape nearly stable. Motion emphasis is reserved for slow cloud drift, micro sakura sway, blurred foreground blossom passes, restrained falling petals, and a very small camera push.
